# v1.8.0

 * Pick the voice AskDash talks with in live talk (10 voices). Changing it during live talk restarts it, so you hear the new voice right away
 * <cy>Savage</c> live talk plays rare pranks on you: it may pause your game mid-run, fake a death sound, rename your attempt or send a fake notification. Never close to your best, and <cy>Live talk: Savage pranks</c> turns them off
 * Live talk stays fresh longer: fewer jokes about the same percents and misclicks, sometimes silence, praise that turns into a roast, totally unrelated fun facts out of boredom, and it calls you a vegetable now and then. <cy>Savage</c> goes right to the edge

# v1.7.0

 * <cy>Live talk</c> (Windows): talk with AskDash by voice while you play. It listens to your microphone, answers out loud, roasts your deaths (walls, chokes near your best, thrown long runs), hypes you mid-run when you pass your best and breaks the silence now and then. Press <cy>Ctrl+Shift+L</c> in a level or the <cg>LIVE</c> button in the pause menu
 * Live talk settings: the language AskDash speaks (<cy>Auto</c> answers in the language you speak), how hard it roasts you, how chatty it is, headphones (to interrupt it by talking) and subtitles (off by default)
 * A short setup quiz on the first launch: your skill, live talk language, roast level, chattiness, headphones and session summaries

# v1.6.1

 * New session summary: stat cards, a chart of where you died over the level and how you did compared to last time
 * The first summary asks if you want them at all, and each summary has a <cy>Don't show again</c> box

# v1.6.0

 * Each level has its own chat, opened when you ask from that level. The <cg>General</c> button switches to the usual chat (can be turned off in settings)
 * Quick questions above the input that fit where you are: in a level, on a level page, in the editor or in menus
 * Your sessions on each level are saved on your computer. The session summary shows how you did last time, and the AI can tell if you are improving (can be turned off in settings)

# v1.5.1

 * Session summary knows where each attempt started: start pos and practice runs are shown separately (like 50-63%), and deaths and the best percent only count attempts from 0%

# v1.5.0

 * Free daily limit, plus two cheap paid plans (the Plans button in the chat). Prices just cover the AI costs
 * You now need to be logged into your GD account. The login is checked with Argon
 * The chat shows how much of your limit is left, right under the title
 * Web search for recent info is now only on paid plans
 * Settings button in the chat, so you don't have to go through the Geode menu

# v1.4.0

 * Open AskDash from any screen with a keybind (<cy>Ctrl+Shift+A</c> by default, can be changed in settings); while playing, the level is paused first
 * AskDash button in the editor pause menu and on the page of your own levels
 * Session summary: after leaving a level, see your attempts, best percent and where you died the most, then ask the AI what to practice (can be turned off in settings)

# v1.3.0

 * Optional <cy>Death Tracker</c> integration: with it installed, the AI sees where you die on the open level and suggests what to practice (can be turned off in settings)

# v1.2.0

 * New buttons: levels of a creator, similar levels, search with filters, map packs, gauntlets, level lists and the level song

 * Link your demonlist.org and Pointercrate usernames in settings to ask about your rank and completions
 * Replies appear while they are being written
 * Chat history is saved between game sessions (can be turned off)
 * Copy button next to replies, Retry button on errors
